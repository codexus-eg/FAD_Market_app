# Google Play store listing text

## English (United States)

### App name
FAD Market

### Short description
Shop CNC machines, spare parts, digital designs, and industrial services.

### Full description
FAD Market gives customers one place to explore CNC machines, spare parts, CNC products, digital designs, accessories, and available industrial services.

Browse product categories, view detailed product information and product options, compare images and prices, add items to your cart, and submit orders directly from your phone.

Key features:
- Browse machines, spare parts, accessories, CNC products, and digital designs
- View separate images, details, and pricing for each product option
- Save items to the cart and complete an order
- Choose available payment methods configured by the business
- Track orders and view invoices
- Manage your customer profile and delivery location
- Contact customer support through in-app chat and attachments
- Submit and follow maintenance service requests
- Arabic and English interface

Product availability, prices, payment methods, delivery, installation, and service coverage may vary by country and are managed by FAD Market.

### Initial release notes
Initial release of FAD Market with product browsing, product options, cart, customer accounts, order tracking, invoices, support chat, and maintenance requests.

أبرز المميزات:
